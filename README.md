## Abstract
The chatbot's AI engine ensures that responses are in simple, easy-to-understand language and that queries are handled appropriately, based on their context. The system’s knowledge base is continuously updated with the latest information from trusted health sources and government databases. The chatbot is accessible via a smartphone or through a toll-free phone number, making it widely available to the target population. A pilot study of the chatbot was conducted with a rural community, resulting in positive feedback and a growing number of users.
The AI Chatbot machine brain makes certain that replies come in simple to understand phrasing and proper actions are taken on specific queries depending on the information. Official health sources and government data are used to update the knowledge database. This can be used in smartphones and also through a toll-free phone call which rural people also have access to. A pilot of the chatbot was done within a rural community and positive results plus an increase in users was scene.
