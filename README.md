## Abstract
The Intelligent Disease Awareness Chatbot is a smart AI-based system developed to provide simple and reliable health information to users. The main purpose of this chatbot is to spread awareness about diseases, their symptoms, prevention methods, and vaccination details. It understands user questions using Natural Language Processing (NLP) and gives clear, easy-to-understand answers in a conversational format.
The chatbot is developed using Python along with technologies such as Rasa or Dialogflow, and it can be connected to web or mobile platforms. It can also support voice features, making it helpful for people who may have difficulty reading or typing. The system uses trusted health sources and government data to ensure the information provided is accurate and up to date.
This chatbot is especially useful for rural and low-resource areas where access to healthcare information may be limited. It helps users get quick guidance without visiting a hospital for basic queries. Overall, the project aims to improve public health awareness, reduce misinformation, and make important health information easily accessible to everyone.
## Introduction
The AI-driven chatbot delivers health information in simple, easy-to-understand language while interpreting user queries based on context to provide accurate and relevant responses. Its knowledge base is continuously updated using trusted health sources and government databases, ensuring reliability and up-to-date information.
The chatbot is accessible via smartphones as well as through a toll-free phone service, making it widely available, especially in rural and low-resource areas. This accessibility allows users with limited internet connectivity to benefit from essential health guidance.
A pilot study conducted in a rural community showed positive outcomes, with users finding the system helpful and easy to use. There was a noticeable increase in user engagement along with improved awareness of disease prevention and healthcare practices. The results indicate that the chatbot is an effective tool for enhancing public health awareness and reaching underserved populations.
## Literature Review
While current solutions like health information portals, and mobile health applications avail useful information, they lack local access toward rural communities especially due to language diversity and internet connectivity dependency. 
Conversational agents based on AI techniques, particularly Dialogflow and Rasa, have been recently applied on healthcare domains for symptom checking and appointment booking. Research has shown that conversational AI could enhance users’ engagement and information recall performance. However, most existing systems are not designed for low-resource environments and/or lack the real-time integration of government health data. 
Our solution leverages state-of-the-art technologies in the aforementioned areas in a coordinated manner that to our knowledge, is novel in the literature.

Programming Languages:
- Python  

Frameworks/Libraries:

- SpeechRecognition / gTTS (Voice processing)

Tools Used:

- Database Systems (MongoDB / PostgreSQL)

The system is deployed on cloud infrastructure to ensure scalability, reliability, and support for both text and voice-based user interaction.
## Results and Discussion
The chatbot effectively answers user questions regarding symptoms of diseases, preventive measures, and the vaccination calendar for diseases. 
Major achievements:
- Responded to health questions with an accuracy of almost 80%
- Increased awareness by about 20% in the pilot areas
- Sent out real-time disease outbreak alerts
The use of SMS and WhatsApp made it very convenient and engaging as it reached even the low internet-penetration areas.
## Limitation
- Relying on the government health data that may or may not be available and accurate
- Lack of expertise in understanding very complex medical questions
- Difficulties in translating languages for less common dialects
- Need for constant updates to be correct
## Future Scope
- Make it available in more regional languages and dialects to reach a wider population
- Include AI for symptom diagnosis based on initial information provided by the user
- Develop offline functionality for areas with limited internet connectivity
- Integrate the app with telemedicine providers for remote consultations.
## Conculusion  
The public health chatbot platform based on NLP and machine learning, and incorporating real-time data, could be a viable solution for improving health literacy in marginalized populations, by providing scalable, timely, and multilingual access to best-quality health information.
